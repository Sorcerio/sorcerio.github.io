Writer, Creative, Software Engineer

I operate across disciplines. I've built software systems, shipped published works, and led teams. Pursuits that look unrelated on the surface, but share a common foundation in architecture and pattern recognition. Whether the medium is code, prose, or people, the challenge is the same: find the efficient structure that serves the goal, then execute on it.

Recognizing that structure exists is one thing. Understanding why it should be assembled a specific way is another. I focus on the reasoning behind the decisions. The "why" that makes systems modular, writing resonant, and teams productive. That principle applies whether I'm building a tool from scratch, designing a player-facing experience, or organizing the people responsible for shipping any of the above.

The short version: quality work demands understanding across every facet of the problem. Achieving that understanding demands a willingness to learn each facet firsthand; not theoretically, but by doing the work.

That's how I move projects forward.

- Brody Childs
