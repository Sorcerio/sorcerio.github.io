TRIPPY HIKE
Wing: Games & Engines   Room: Gallery I
Tags: iOS, Swift, GameKit, StoreKit, Game Development
================================================================

Trippy Hike was a casual mobile game designed, developed, and shipped entirely by me. The program was written by hand in the time before agentic systems existed and was based upon a significant amount of research into Apple's GameKit documentation. Trippy Hike implemented native libraries including GameKit, GameCenter, and StoreKit to deliver an expandable and monetized experience from concept to App Store.

Trippy Hike as it existed is now no longer available on the Apple App Store. However, Trippy Hike 2 is on my list projects to revisit in a multi-platform capacity.
