URUK: CITY OF CONCRETE TEETH
Wing: Illegal Astronauts   Room: Short Stories
================================================================

On the Syndicate Corporations city planet of Uruk, veterans of the 1st Syndicate-Outcast war now work as mercenaries and solos for corporations, splinter groups, and individuals. Garrick, the last of the Screaming Fists, is among this abandoned legion. Today, something of great wealth falls from the sky to land before him. Its presence both a boon of potential wealth and a magnet for danger. He is in a race against time to offload it before he becomes the next victim.
