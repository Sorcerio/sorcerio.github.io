NON DEOS. NEC DOMINIS.
Wing: Illegal Astronauts   Room: Short Stories
================================================================

What do you do with the mercenaries when the war is over. Atilon and the rest of Sol Company once fought under contract for the Syndicated Corporations. Now, the 1st Syndicate-Outcast War has ended in a rough stalemate. The fighting concluded and the mercenaries were shipped back into the Core Systems. Their members locked into a zero gravity colosseum to fight. Each battle lowering Sol Company's debts to their old employers. But, Atilon asks: is this how a warrior dies?
