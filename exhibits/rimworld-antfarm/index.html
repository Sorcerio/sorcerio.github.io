THE RIMWORLD ANTFARM
Wing: Games & Engines   Room: Gallery I
Tags: csharp, python, rimworld
================================================================

The Rimworld Antfarm is an autonomous AI colony administrator for RimWorld utilizing any local or API accessible LLM. The program is a demonstration of wrapping a complex, stateful simulation into a structured API that is driven with an LLM agent loop. The system itself being allegorical to complex human structures like city planning and resource management infrastructure.

This means that the colony runs itself. The AI reads game state, formulates plans, issues commands, and adapts tick-to-tick without human input. The goal is not to win: it's to live long enough to foster a story and collapse in spectacle in the same manner as watching an ant farm develop and, eventually, collapse.
