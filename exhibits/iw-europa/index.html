ICE WAR: EUROPA
Wing: Illegal Astronauts   Room: Short Stories
================================================================

Clean water has come to an end on planet Earth. But, our solar system sustains us. Operations to collect and return ice from Europa have begun and, with it, conflict. The Berg is a warzone. A group of Galileo Prospecting Group surface operators emerge from their captured bunker to patrol under the watchful eye of Jupiter. They are born to freeze. Beyond is only ice and realization.
